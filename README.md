## What is a faceless channel

A YouTube channel where you never appear on camera. The content is narration, music, animation, or stock footage. AI made the production side cheap: a script becomes a voiced, illustrated, edited video without a crew. What it did not make cheap is the part that always mattered, picking a niche and writing something people want to watch.

## Niches that work

Rough RPM ranges (revenue per 1,000 monetized views) as reported by creators. Kids content runs low because made-for-kids videos lose personalized ads.

| Niche | Typical RPM | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Kids songs and nursery rhymes | $0.10 to $3 | Huge volume, low RPM, very repeatable |
| Bedtime stories and fairy tales | $0.10 to $3 | Calm pacing, strong watch time |
| Bible and scripture stories | $3 to $8 | Loyal adult audience, underserved in many languages |
| Lofi and ambient music | $3 to $8 | Long sessions, general audience |
| History and mythology | $3 to $8 | Script quality decides everything |
| Space and science explainers | $3 to $8 | Evergreen, competitive |
| Finance and business explainers | $8 and up | Highest RPM, highest content bar |
| ASMR (AI-generated) | $3 to $8 | Exploding since native-audio video models |

A longer ranked list with difficulty notes: [13 faceless YouTube channel ideas](https://www.tubetube.io/faceless-youtube-channel-ideas).

## The toolchain

### All-in-one (script to finished video)

- [TubeTube](https://www.tubetube.io) - Turns lyrics or a story into a finished long-form video: music or narration, consistent characters, scenes, final cut and thumbnail in one run. Disclosure: this is us.
- [Pictory](https://pictory.ai) - Text to video built on stock footage, strong for listicle and explainer formats.
- [videos.social](https://videos.social/?utm_source=awesome-faceless-youtube&utm_medium=directory&utm_campaign=listing-wave-d) - Turns blogs, PDFs, and prompts into an editable faceless draft (script, scenes, voiceover).
- [InVideo AI](https://invideo.io) - Prompt to video with stock assets and voiceover.
- [Revid](https://www.revid.ai) - Short-form focused, TikTok and Shorts pipelines.

### Video generation models (raw access)

- [Kling](https://kling.ai) - Strong motion, good price. The 2.6 Pro tier is a common default.
- [Google Veo 3.1](https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow) - Native audio, cinematic quality, 8 second cap per clip. Comparison of ways to access it: [Veo 3 alternatives](https://www.tubetube.io/veo-3-alternative).
- [Hailuo (MiniMax)](https://hailuoai.video) - Best value per second of finished video in many tests.
- [Runway](https://runwayml.com) - The veteran, strong editing integrations.

### Voice and audio

- [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io) - The standard for narration voices and dubbing.
- [Suno](https://suno.com) - Full songs with vocals from a text prompt.
- [Mubert](https://mubert.com) - Royalty-free generated background music.

### Writing

- [Claude](https://claude.ai) and [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) - Script drafts, hooks, title iterations. The output is a draft, not a script. Edit it.

### Channel operations

- [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com) - Keyword and competitor research.
- [TubeBuddy](https://www.tubebuddy.com) - Bulk metadata, A/B thumbnails.

## YouTube policies you must know

- [Inauthentic content policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392) - Mass-produced template spam is demonetized. Original scripts, varied scenes and consistent style keep you clear.
- [AI content disclosure](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491) - Realistic AI-generated content must be labeled.
- [Made for kids](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9528076) - Kids content loses personalized ads, which is why kids RPM is low.
